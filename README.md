One line, roasting a smart fridge that refuses to open after midnight — written as its official apology note:

> Official apology from your fridge: it will not open after midnight. The leftovers filed a noise complaint at 11:59, and frankly, they have a point.

`main.py` writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` (UTF-8, 147 characters, one line).
